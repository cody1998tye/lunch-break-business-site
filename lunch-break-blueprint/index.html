The Lunch Break Blueprint: Your First $500 Online

This guide takes you from zero to your first $500 online, on lunch breaks, from your phone. Every chapter ends with one thing you do that same day.

Who it's for
  • You work a full-time job
  • You have a family that needs you when you get home
  • You've tried to make money online before, maybe more than once, and it didn't stick
  • You don't need another idea. You need one thing finished and paid for.
Who I am
  • I'm Cody. I lead an operations team at a furniture warehouse, drive DoorDash some nights, and have four boys.
  • I spent years on affiliate marketing, dropshipping, ebooks and YouTube rabbit holes with nothing to show for it.
  • In December 2025 I started posting on Threads in 30-minute windows. I sold my first offer 11 times before it existed and delivered it two days early.
  • I built everything in this guide from my phone, on breaks.

What you'll have by the end

  • One offer, picked from what you already know
  • A way to get seen in 30 minutes a day
  • That offer sold before you build it
  • Your first $500, and a plan to do it again

How to use it

  • Read one chapter per lunch break. Most take about 15 minutes.
  • Do the task at the end of the chapter before you read the next one.
  • Go in order. The order is the system.
  • Two ways to do every task. Do it by hand with the worksheet, or copy the AI prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat.
