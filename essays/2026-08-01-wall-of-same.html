Wall Of Same

I was scrolling Threads and everything sounded like the same post, just different fonts. Then I opened my drafts. I was looking at the same thing.

My first thought was I needed to find my niche. Or to post more. Neither one is the real problem.

When you let AI write your posts, it generates the average of your voice. The sharp take you'd only say to one person? That one gets smoothed out.

Tone is style. Anyone can copy style. Only you know what you actually think.

When you write what you actually believe, the right person reads it and feels found. That's what makes them follow you. It's what makes them buy later.

Your thinking is the thing nobody can replicate. Start writing like it.
