Ai Voice

I tell AI my voice is conversational, direct, and blunt. That's supposed to be enough to sound like me.

And in a way it works. AI can absolutely match a tone when you describe it.

Give it "conversational" and you get conversational. Direct? You get direct.

The problem is every other creator on this app gave it the same description.

The word "conversational" describes a category. Your voice is what you do inside that category that nobody else does quite the same way.

Your voice lives in specifics. The exact opinion you'd say out loud that most people soften. The way your thoughts sound when you're explaining something you actually care about.

When you describe your tone to AI, it finds the average of everyone who described themselves the same way. That average is not you.

The move is to give AI your actual writing. Your posts, your notes, the way you actually talk.

Then the instructions change. Instead of "write conversationally," you give it rules pulled from your actual posts. "I never warm up before the point." "I always start with the moment."

That's your voice. Specific beats vague every time.

Most people are still typing "conversational and direct" into a prompt and wondering why it sounds like everyone else.

The ones whose content actually sounds like them stopped describing their voice and started showing it.
