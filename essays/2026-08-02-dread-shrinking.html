Dread Shrinking

Opener

I felt it again this Sunday. The weight sitting on my chest before the week even started.

Back to work tomorrow, another week of someone else's thing. But this time there was something sitting next to it that wasn't there a year ago.

Skeleton

  1. [Open in scene] — The Sunday dread, named and specific. The familiar weight. But something else is there now too.
  1. [The turn] — A year ago it was just the dread. Not wanting to leave your family, not wanting to clock in for a boss that doesnt care.

Now its different you are building something for yourself alongside it. Something that is 100% yours.

  1. [Extract the principle] — Something worth getting to started growing next to it. That's what shrunk the dread.

Anticipation showed up. That's where everything changed.

  1. [Why it transfers] — The dread was always information. It was the pull to build your own thing, to start building a life outside of the "norm". Something that gives you more money and more importantly time with your family.

The problem is nobody told you that. So you spent years thinking something was wrong with you for feeling it.

  1. [Close] — The dread isn't meant to keep you stuck, its your nervous system telling you its time to start doing this for yourself.
