Staying Is the System

I spent a long time telling myself I just hadn't found the right system yet.

One more course. Another strategy. The right combination was out there somewhere.

I kept stacking. More guides, more frameworks. Sometimes running two at once.

I was too busy building the right system to actually use one.

Eventually I stopped. Dropped everything and went back to the most basic version of this I could find. Me and a notes app.

Things started moving. Content that had felt stuck for weeks started clicking. I'd been spending more time looking for the system than running one.

Most people leave their system right before it starts working.

Staying is the part they skip.
