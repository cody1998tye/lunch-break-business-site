3 Offer Formats That Actually Work for Beginners

You're worrying too much on how to package your offer.

There are only 3 options when starting out:

The PDF guide.

One specific problem. Solved in writing. 10 to 20 pages. $17 to $47.

I know it sounds simple. That's the point.

Your first offer just needs to be useful to one specific person. A PDF that solves one painful problem will outsell a 12-module course every time.

Create it in a weekend. Put it up. Done.

The live workshop.

One 60 to 90 minute session where you walk someone through solving a problem you already know how to solve.

You can sell this before you even build it. That's what makes it the fastest path to a first dollar. No recording, no editing. Just a link and a clear promise.

I sold mine before I finished the outline.

The template.

A done-for-you resource. A spreadsheet or a Notion doc. Something they plug in and use immediately.

People pay for time saved more than they pay for knowledge gained. A template that takes them 10 minutes to use instead of 10 hours to build themselves sells because the value is obvious the second they see it.

No explaining needed. The result is right there.

You don't need to pick the right format.

You need to pick one and find out if what you know actually solves the problem you think it does. That's all the first offer is for.

The platform, the pricing. All of that comes after someone pays you.

One format. One problem. One person. Start there.
