AI Authorship Problem

Opener

I was scrolling Threads and everything sounded the same. Different accounts, same post. Then I opened my drafts. I was looking at the same thing.

You ever scroll threads and everything feels identical? Just the same one liners regurgitated from hundreds of accounts? I call this "The Wall of Same"

Skeleton

  1. [Open in anchor] — I was scrolling Threads and everything sounded the same. Different accounts, same post. Then I opened my drafts. I was looking at the same thing. You ever scroll threads and everything feels identical? Just the same one liners regurgitated from hundreds of accounts? I call this "The Wall of Same"
  1. [State the default frame] — AI helps you make content faster. And it does. That part's true.
  1. [What the frame makes invisible] — When AI generates the content, it generates the average of everything on the internet. The average of everyone who came before you.
  1. [The flip] — one sentence on its own line.

It's an authorship problem.

  1. [Walk the new frame] — Your thoughts go in, structured content comes out. AI as the system, you as the source. That's the version that sounds like you. Because it is.
  1. [The leverage] — Content gets easier. And you stop blending in. Not because you found a new idea, but because you finally put your actual self behind the old one.
  1. [Close] — one line. Hand it back to the reader.
